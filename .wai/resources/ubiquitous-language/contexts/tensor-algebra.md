# Context: Tensor Algebra

## Key Terms
- **Manifold**: A topological space that locally resembles Euclidean space.
- **Abstract Index**: A label for a tensor slot that represents its variance without binding to specific coordinates.
- **Canonicalization**: The process of reducing a tensor expression to its unique standard form based on symmetries.
- **TExpr**: A typed expression layer that validates slot counts and manifold membership.
