# Context: Tensor Algebra

## Key Terms
- **Manifold**: A topological space that locally resembles Euclidean space.
- **VBundle (Vector Bundle)**: A collection of vector spaces parameterized by the manifold (e.g., Tangent Bundle).
- **Abstract Index**: A label for a tensor slot used in abstract index notation (Penrose notation) to represent contractions and slot positions without choosing a specific basis or coordinate system.
- **Coordinate Index**: A concrete index (integer or specific label) referring to a component within a chosen Chart or Basis.
- **Chart**: A coordinate system mapping a region of the manifold to Euclidean space.
- **Basis**: A set of linearly independent vectors (or covectors) at each point of a manifold or VBundle used to express tensors in components.
- **Canonicalization**: The process of reducing a tensor expression to its unique standard form based on symmetries.
- **TExpr**: A typed expression layer that validates slot counts and manifold membership.
